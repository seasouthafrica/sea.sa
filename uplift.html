Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you apply

Who can apply for this programme? +
Youth aged 18–35 who want to build digital income skills. Whether you're a student, recent graduate, job seeker, or aspiring entrepreneur — if you have ambition and access to a device, you qualify.
Is the programme fully online? +
Yes, 100% online, delivered through our Learning Management System. Access it from any device — laptop, tablet, or smartphone — and learn at your own pace from anywhere.
Do I need prior experience or qualifications? +
No prior experience is required. We start from the basics and build up progressively — all you need is a willingness to learn, a device, and an internet connection.
Will I receive support during the programme? +
Yes. You'll have access to facilitator support plus a peer community of fellow participants, so you're never learning alone.
How long does it take to complete? +
The programme is fully self-paced. Most participants finish in 6–12 weeks studying part-time, with no deadlines or expiry dates.
Will I actually build a portfolio? +
Absolutely — portfolio development runs through every module. By the end, you'll have a professional portfolio website and client-ready work to show immediately.

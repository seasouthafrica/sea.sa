Empowering local enterprises to access big markets

About the programme

Open the door to real market opportunities

At the Social Enterprise Academy, we design Supplier Development Programmes that connect high-potential local entrepreneurs with real market opportunities.

Whether you are a corporate looking to diversify your supply chain or a social entrepreneur seeking to scale your business, our programmes offer structured support, business readiness training and direct buyer engagement—helping to unlock long-term, inclusive economic growth.

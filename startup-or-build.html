Building Your Social Enterprise

Skills training and hands-on practice to start your social project in your community, step by step.

About the programme

About the Programme

The programme helps participants to start social projects, combining skills training with the practical experience of starting up your social project in your community step-by-step.

  • Clearly define your social enterprise idea and the social impact you wish to have
  • Apply a range of business tools and skills to enable you to establish a sustainable and thriving social enterprise
  • Test the viability of your ideas through market research
  • Engage more confidently with and ensure the participation of the community your social enterprise aims to serve
  • Create a living business plan to support the development and management of your social enterprise
  • Identify what sort of leader you need to be to lead your social enterprise
